How to check your Claude usage limits (session, weekly & Claude Code)

Updated 17 November 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: on claude.ai open Settings → Usage. You'll see your 5-hour session limit and your weekly limits as progress bars, each with its next reset time. In Claude Code, run /usage. Claude.ai, Claude Code and Claude Desktop all count towards the same limits.

1. Check your limits on claude.ai

  1. Open claude.ai and sign in.
  2. Click your name or initials in the bottom-left corner and choose Settings.
  3. Open the Usage tab.

The Usage page is available on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans. It shows:

What you seeWhat it means
Current sessionHow much of your 5-hour session limit you've used and when it resets.
Weekly – all modelsYour weekly limit across all models and the time it resets.
Weekly – a specific modelOn some plans there's a separate weekly limit for the largest model.
Usage creditsIf enabled, how much you've spent beyond your plan and your monthly cap.

2. Check your limits in Claude Code

If you use Claude Code with your claude.ai subscription, type /usage in a session. It shows a breakdown of your plan usage with suggestions for using it more efficiently.

Keep in mind that /usage works from your local session history – usage from other computers, from claude.ai or from your phone isn't included there. For the full picture, use Settings → Usage on claude.ai. Usage credits can be managed in Claude Code with /usage-credits.

3. How the Claude limits work

The 5-hour session limit

On paid plans, a session starts with your first message and resets in full five hours later. If you start working at 9:00, your session resets at 14:00 – no matter how much you used in between.

The weekly limits

Weekly limits reset at a fixed time each week that's assigned to your account – not on Monday for everyone. The exact day and hour is shown in Settings → Usage.

One shared pool

Chatting on claude.ai, coding with Claude Code and using Claude Desktop all draw from the same limits. A long Claude Code session in the morning can leave less for chatting in the afternoon. Anthropic may also adjust limits over time to manage capacity, so the exact amount of work that fits in a session can change.

4. What to do when you hit the limit

  • Wait for the reset. Settings → Usage tells you exactly when your session or week resets.
  • Use usage credits. On paid plans with usage credits enabled, you can keep working and pay for the extra usage.
  • Start a new conversation for a new topic. Long conversations use more of your limit with every message, because Claude rereads the whole context.
  • Use a lighter model for simple tasks and keep the largest model for the hard ones.
  • Plan heavy work – check how much of your session is left before starting a large Claude Code task.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I see my Claude usage limits?

On claude.ai under Settings → Usage, available on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans.

When does the 5-hour session limit reset?

Five hours after the first message of the session.

When do weekly limits reset?

At a fixed time each week assigned to your account – check Settings → Usage for yours.

Does Claude Code have separate limits?

No. Claude Code, claude.ai and Claude Desktop share the same limits. /usage in Claude Code only shows usage from your local sessions.

Can I see usage on the free plan?

The free plan has limits too, but the Usage page with session and weekly bars is part of the paid plans.

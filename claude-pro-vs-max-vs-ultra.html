Claude Pro vs Max vs Ultra: how much usage do you get?

Updated 18 November 2026 · 6 min read

EnglishSlovenčinaČeštinaDeutschItalianoFrançais
Short answer: Pro ($20/month) has a 5-hour session limit and a weekly limit. Max ($100/month) gives you 5× Pro usage – every 5 hours and every week. Ultra ($200/month, formerly Max 20x) gives you 10× Pro usage every week and, once your account is verified, no 5-hour limit. If you only run out now and then, usage credits on Pro can be cheaper than upgrading.

What the plans have in common

  • Claude Code is included in Pro, Max and Ultra – one subscription for the claude.ai chat, Claude Desktop and Claude Code.
  • One shared pool: chatting, coding in Claude Code and using the desktop app all count towards the same limits.
  • Weekly limits apply across all models and reset at a fixed time assigned to your account.
  • On Max and Ultra there's also a separate weekly limit for the largest model, so you can't spend your whole week on it.

Which plan do you need?

Anthropic doesn't publish exact message counts – usage depends on conversation length, attachments, tools and the model. The best guide is how you actually hit the limits:

If you…Then
Rarely hit a limitPro is enough.
Hit the limit a few times a monthStay on Pro and turn on usage credits – you pay only for the extra usage, with a monthly cap you set.
Hit the 5-hour limit most daysMax: five times more usage per session and per week.
Run out of the weekly limit before the week endsMax if you need up to 5× more; Ultra if you need more than that.
Work in long, uninterrupted sessions (e.g. all-day Claude Code)Ultra: verified accounts have no 5-hour limit, only the weekly one.

Before upgrading, check whether you're simply using more than you need – long conversations, big attachments and high effort levels drain any plan quickly. See Why do I hit the Claude usage limit so fast?

Ultra: what "no 5-hour limit" means

Ultra replaced the former Max 20x plan – existing Max 20x subscribers were moved to Ultra at the same price. On Ultra, the 5-hour session limit disappears once your account is verified. Anthropic verifies Ultra accounts automatically based on account and payment history; there's nothing you need to do. If Settings → Usage on claude.ai doesn't show a Current session bar, your account is verified. The weekly limit still applies.

Measure your usage before you decide

The cheapest plan is the one that fits how you really work. Watch your usage for a week or two:

  • How often does your 5-hour session reach 100 %?
  • On which day does your weekly limit run out – or does it last the week?
  • How much of it is Claude Code?

Settings → Usage on claude.ai shows the current state, but not the history. That's where a usage history helps.

Frequently asked questions

How much more usage does Claude Max give than Pro?

Max gives five times the Pro plan's usage, both every 5 hours and every week.

What is the difference between Claude Max and Ultra?

Max costs $100/month and gives 5× Pro usage with a 5-hour limit. Ultra costs $200/month, gives 10× Pro usage every week and has no 5-hour limit for verified accounts.

What happened to Claude Max 20x?

Max 20x was renamed Ultra. Existing Max 20x subscribers were moved to Ultra automatically at the same price.

Is Claude Code included in Pro?

Yes. Pro, Max and Ultra all include Claude Code, and it shares the same limits as the claude.ai chat and the desktop app.

Can I get more usage without upgrading?

Yes. On paid plans you can turn on usage credits and keep working after you hit a limit, paying only for the extra usage up to a monthly cap you set.

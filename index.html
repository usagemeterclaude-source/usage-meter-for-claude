Frequently asked questions

How do I check my Claude usage limit?

On claude.ai you can open Settings → Usage. Usage Meter for Claude shows the same numbers all the time: the percentage is on the toolbar icon and the popup shows the current session, the weekly limit and when each one resets – without opening any settings page.

What is the Claude 5-hour session limit?

Claude Pro and Max plans have a usage limit per session that resets 5 hours after the session starts, plus a weekly limit across all models. The extension shows both, including per-model weekly limits, and tells you when a new session starts.

Does it work with Claude Code?

Yes. Claude Code uses the same limits as claude.ai, so its usage is included in the session and weekly numbers – also usage from the desktop app and your phone. The extension refreshes in the background even when claude.ai isn't open.

Is it free?

Yes. The usage on the icon, the popup, notifications, the run-out forecast, usage on claude.ai and 7 days of history are free. PRO (€0.99/month or €7.99/year, 7-day free trial) adds 30 and 60-day history, several accounts at once and CSV export.

Which Claude plans are supported?

Claude Pro, Max and Team. The free Claude plan doesn't provide usage data, so there is nothing to show.

Is my data safe?

The extension only reads your own usage percentages from claude.ai using the session you're already signed in with. It never reads your conversations, and nothing is sent to the developer – everything stays in your browser. No analytics, no tracking.

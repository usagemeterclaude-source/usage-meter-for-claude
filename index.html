Usage Meter for Claude

A browser extension that shows your Claude.ai usage limits right in the toolbar.

Get it forGoogle Chrome Get it forMicrosoft Edge
  • Current session and weekly limit on the toolbar icon
  • Reset times and a forecast of when you'll run out
  • Usage right on claude.ai – under the message box and in the sidebar
  • Notifications at chosen thresholds, when a limit resets and before a free reset expires
  • History and weekly overview, available in 27 languages
  • PRO: longer history, several accounts at once, CSV export

Works with Claude Pro, Max and Team plans (the free plan doesn't provide usage data).
